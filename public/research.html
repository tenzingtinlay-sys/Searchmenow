How Searchmenow ranks evidence

01 · Relevance

Exact title and query-token matches rank above loosely related mentions.

02 · Source quality

Structured public datasets and established research or developer sources receive a documented baseline weight.

03 · Corroboration

Repeated evidence across independent sources helps prioritize a result; duplicates are merged.

04 · Transparency

Every result links to its source. Failed sources are labelled unavailable, never silently treated as “no result.”
